Our Story Began in the Heart of Geneva

In 1898, master watchmaker Viktor Dekhkanov opened a small atelier in Geneva's historic old town. His vision was simple yet profound: to create timepieces that would transcend generations, each one a masterpiece of mechanical artistry.

What started as a modest workshop has evolved into one of the world's most respected luxury watch manufacturers. Yet, despite our growth, we've never lost sight of Viktor's original philosophy: every watch that bears the Dekhkanov name must represent the pinnacle of horological excellence.

Today, our watches are worn by discerning collectors, world leaders, and connoisseurs who appreciate the marriage of traditional craftsmanship and innovative engineering. Each piece tells a story – not just of time passing, but of moments captured, memories preserved, and legacies created.

"A watch is more than a tool for keeping time. It is a companion through life's most precious moments, a legacy to be passed down through generations." - Viktor Dekhkanov, 1898

Our Journey Through Time

1898

The Beginning

Viktor Dekhkanov opens his first atelier in Geneva, establishing the foundation of what would become a legendary watchmaking house.

1925

First Complications

Launch of our first perpetual calendar movement, establishing Dekhkanov as a master of complex mechanisms.

1952

Global Recognition

Dekhkanov timepieces worn at the coronation of Queen Elizabeth II, cementing our status among royalty and dignitaries.

1978

Innovation Era

Introduction of our revolutionary ultra-thin movement, just 2.4mm thick, setting new standards in watchmaking.

2005

Modern Heritage

Opening of our state-of-the-art manufacture, combining traditional craftsmanship with cutting-edge technology.

2025

Future Vision

Continuing our legacy with sustainable practices and innovative materials while honoring 127 years of excellence.
