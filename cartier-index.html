The House of Cartier

Founded in Paris in 1847 by Louis-François Cartier, the Maison has defined luxury for generations. We are the jeweler of kings and the king of jewelers, creating pieces that transcend time and trend.

From the legendary Tank watch, inspired by the Renault tanks of World War I, to the graceful curves of the Ballon Bleu, each Cartier timepiece is a work of art. Our watches are not simply instruments of time—they are expressions of individuality, statements of style, and symbols of excellence.
